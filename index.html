The problem

Small teams lose time and money in the inbox

When one person handles orders, packing and support, every message looks urgent. Three things tend to go wrong.

Everything looks urgent

Messages from chats, email and DMs pile up. A double charge sits next to “is COD available?”, and the important one gets answered late.

Tamil and Tanglish are hard

Customers write the way they speak: “Rendu thadava charge aaiduchu”. Many generic tools are built around English and can miss the meaning.

Goodwill coupons get misused

Handing out coupons to everyone who complains is costly. Handing them out to nobody loses customers. You need a fair, explainable rule.

How it works

From message to a safe decision in four steps

  1. 1

    Paste ticket

    Drop in the customer’s message. Phone numbers and emails are masked straight away.

  2. 2

    AI triage

    Priority, a short summary, the right team, suggested steps and a draft reply in the customer’s own style.

  3. 3

    Fault check

    Was the seller at fault, or did the customer just have a doubt? Unsure results go to the owner.

  4. 4

    Capped reward

    Only seller-fault issues can earn a small coupon, within your limits, after the customer confirms and you approve.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you have to set up

English, Tamil & Tanglish

Built-in keyword rules understand all three. Optionally connect your own AI endpoint.

Explainable fault check

Every ticket shows Seller fault, Customer doubt or Unclear, with a one-line reason.

Suggested steps

Three or four steps for the team, clearly labelled as suggestions the team must confirm.

Draft replies

A reply in the same language style as the ticket. It never promises refunds or coupons.

Ticket board

Six stages from New to Closed, ticket age, and a warning when a High ticket waits too long.

Abuse-safe rewards

Coupon caps, a monthly limit per customer, repeat-complaint flags, 30-day expiry and an audit log.

Privacy by default

Phones and emails are masked in the UI and before any text reaches an AI service. No API key in the code.

Live dashboard

Priority, team, status, fault split and reward spend versus your monthly limit.

Export & reset

Download tickets as CSV and reset the demo data whenever you like.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Does TicketPilot decide refunds for me?

No. AI suggestions must be confirmed by a human, especially for refunds and money decisions. The tool prepares the facts, and you decide.

How does the fault check work?

It reads the ticket and sets Seller fault, Customer doubt or Unclear, with a one-line reason. If confidence is below 60%, the result is forced to Unclear and sent to the owner, and no reward is possible.

What stops customers from abusing rewards?

Rewards need seller fault, Medium or High priority, a monthly limit per customer, fewer than four complaints, customer confirmation and your approval. Coupons expire after 30 days, and every decision is in the audit log.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser (localStorage). There is no server and no account. Clearing site data removes it.

Can I use a real AI model?

Yes. In Settings, enter the URL of your own small serverless proxy. It keeps the API key secret and returns JSON. Only masked text is sent. If the call fails, TicketPilot falls back to offline mode.

Why is there no API key box?

Anything in a browser can be read by visitors. Pasting a secret key into client-side code would leak it, so TicketPilot never asks for one.
